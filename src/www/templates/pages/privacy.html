Aggregate visit statistics

The website records aggregate pageviews to understand which pages people use and identify errors. Each record contains the page path, time, response status, referring website, campaign labels, and a bot flag. These records contain no cookies, visitor identifiers, IP addresses, full referring URLs, or browsing sessions.

Statistics are stored in Google Cloud BigQuery and expire after 180 days. They cannot identify returning visitors or reconstruct an individual's browsing history.

Google Analytics, only with your consent

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, this website and my course sites (mlops-coding-course.fmind.dev and agentops-open-course.fmind.dev) load Google Analytics 4 to measure visits: pages viewed, time on page, referring website, device and browser type, and the approximate location Google derives from your connection. Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID> on fmind.dev for up to two years to recognize a returning browser. Advertising features and Google signals are disabled, and Google keeps event data for two months. Google processes this data under its Google Analytics terms and may transfer it to the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Nothing loads before you accept. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and you can change your choice at any time from Cookie settings in the footer; rejecting also deletes this website's Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is stored only in your browser.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, Google Calendar, and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Sending an email or booking mentoring shares the information you choose to provide with the relevant service.
