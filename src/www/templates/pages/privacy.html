Aggregate visit statistics

The website records aggregate pageviews to understand which pages people use and identify errors. Each record contains the page path, time, response status, referring website, campaign labels, and a bot flag. These records contain no cookies, visitor identifiers, IP addresses, full referring URLs, or browsing sessions.

Statistics are stored in Google Cloud BigQuery and expire after 180 days. They cannot identify returning visitors or reconstruct an individual's browsing history.

External services
