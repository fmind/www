## Highlights

- [xai-org/grok-build](https://github.com/xai-org/grok-build): xAI open-sources Grok Build, a Rust-based full-screen terminal coding agent that edits files, executes commands, and coordinates subagents via the Agent Client Protocol.
- [The most controversial rewrite in history just shipped...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXSvKcLovAk): Fireship explores the highly debated decision of the Bun JavaScript runtime to rewrite its core codebase from Zig to Rust, highlighting the technical trade-offs between performance and safety.
