- [Lessons From Red-teaming Auto-review, OpenAI's Blocking Monitor](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/dbStc8ZFESqfihwAd/lessons-from-red-teaming-auto-review-openai-s-blocking-1): Uncovers 11 exploit vectors that bypass OpenAI Codex's sandbox and Auto-review monitor, including prompt injections and stale-approval timing attacks, prompting OpenAI fixes.
