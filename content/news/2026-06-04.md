## Highlights

- [Gemma 4 12B: The Developer Guide](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemma-4-12b-the-developer-guide): Google has released Gemma 4 12B, a medium-sized, encoder-free multimodal open model capable of natively ingesting audio and video for local AI development.
- [Bringing Gemma 4 12B to your Laptop: Unlocking Local, Agentic Workflows with Google AI Edge](https://developers.googleblog.com/bringing-gemma-4-12b-to-your-laptop-unlocking-local-agentic-workflows-with-google-ai-edge): Google AI Edge has optimized Gemma 4 12B to run agentic, multimodal AI workflows directly on standard laptops with 16GB of RAM, enabling offline Python execution and dictation.
- [litert-community/gemma-4-12B-it-litert-lm on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/gemma-4-12B-it-litert-lm): The official repository for the LiteRT-compatible instruction-tuned Gemma 4 12B model, enabling on-device execution of local agentic workflows.

## More

- [Getting Started with Managed Agents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0YXe7u-i1qU): A video guide by Google for Developers showcasing how to build, deploy, and scale custom agentic applications using Managed Agents in Google AI Studio and the Gemini API.
- [Automate M365 to Google Workspace Migrations with ADK multi-agents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9BwFgEFVLOk): Google Cloud Tech has published a video demonstration on using multi-agent systems built with the Agent Development Kit to automate enterprise migrations from Microsoft 365 to Google Workspace.
