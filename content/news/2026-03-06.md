## Highlights

- [OpenAI releases GPT-5.4](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/): The new model features native computer-use capabilities, 1M token context window, and significantly improved reasoning and coding performance.
- [Clinejection supply chain attack](https://grith.ai/blog/clinejection-when-your-ai-tool-installs-another): A major vulnerability allowed an attacker to compromise 4,000 developer machines by injecting instructions into a GitHub issue title read by an AI triage bot.
- [Google introduces Canvas in AI Mode](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-canvas-writing-coding/): A new dynamic workspace in Google Search that supports persistent creative writing and coding tasks, including building interactive tool prototypes.

- [Anthropic research on AI's labor market impacts](https://www.anthropic.com/research/labor-market-impacts): A new study introduces "observed exposure" to measure real-world AI impact on jobs, finding limited effects on unemployment to date but a slight slowdown in youth hiring.
- [Burger King's new AI chatbot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lsqR0oxtvOw): Burger King is deploying a new AI chatbot designed to assist employees with operations and improve customer "friendliness."
