## Highlights

- [DeepSeek Drops Enhanced Coding Agent With Aim to Rival Anthropic](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-01/deepseek-drops-enhanced-coding-agent-with-aim-to-rival-anthropic): Open-sources dsh-superagent under the MIT license, an autonomous coding agent prototype from the DeepSeek Harness team built on a Router-Worker architecture that continuously monitors GitHub repositories and Feishu group chats to triage issues, delegate asynchronous tickets across parallel isolated worker sessions, and open pull requests.
- [We’ve received our first response from the Project Suncatcher prototype satellite](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response): Completes the first orbital AI inference on a data-center-class TPU aboard Google's October 2026 prototype satellite, executing telemetry and text generation prompts on an onboard Gemma 4 open model during a Svalbard ground-station pass ahead of thermal and radiation stress tests for a 2027 twin-satellite interconnect.
- [Key Takeaways from the AI Threat Readiness Survey 2026](https://www.wiz.io/blog/ai-threat-readiness-survey-report-2026): Surveys 100 senior IT and security leaders to reveal that while 55% of enterprises deploy autonomous agents across MCP and RAG architectures and 31% run over 25 production AI systems, only 17% maintain a complete AI asset inventory and 52% uncovered unauthorized shadow AI over the past year, with sensitive data access (70%), unmonitored decisions (54%), and overprivileged identities (53%) topping agentic risk concerns.
