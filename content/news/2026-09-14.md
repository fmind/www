- [Introducing CUDA Rust: Two Tracks for Writing GPU Kernels](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/introducing-cuda-rust-two-tracks-for-writing-gpu-kernels): NVIDIA introduces CUDA Rust for native GPU kernels in Rust across two tracks: cuda-oxide for fine-grained SIMT kernels compiling to PTX and cutile-rs for tile-based execution in stable Rust.
- [A2A CLI: The Official Command-Line Interface for A2A Agents](https://github.com/a2aproject/a2a-cli): The A2A Project releases a2a-cli, the official command-line interface to discover, message, manage, and inspect Agent2Agent protocol-compatible agents directly from terminal environments and AI coding harnesses.
- [Graph Engineering with Google ADK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mzr7byMFy_4): Google Cloud Tech publishes a guide to graph engineering with the Agent Development Kit (ADK), replacing fragile monolithic prompts with parallel fan-out and deterministic aggregation in multi-agent systems.
- [Dario Amodei — We Must Pace the Frontier](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier): Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei advocates for deliberately pacing the rate of frontier AI capabilities advancement to allow safety research, defense mechanisms, and institutional governance sufficient time to mitigate catastrophic risks.
