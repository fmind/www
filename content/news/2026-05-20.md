## Highlights

- [Gemini 3.5: frontier intelligence with action](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/): Google launches Gemini 3.5 Flash, a new model family that outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks while running 4x faster than other frontier models, now available in the Gemini app, Antigravity, and the Gemini API.
- [Introducing Gemini Omni](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/): Google introduces Gemini Omni Flash, a new multimodal model that can generate and conversationally edit high-quality videos from any combination of text, image, video, and audio inputs, rolling out globally to Google AI subscribers.
- [Introducing Managed Agents in the Gemini API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/managed-agents-gemini-api/): Google launches Managed Agents in the Gemini API, letting developers spin up a full agentic environment in an isolated Linux sandbox with a single API call using the Antigravity agent powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash.
