## Highlights

- [Building Claude Code with Boris Cherny](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=julbw1JuAz0): Boris Cherny discusses the architecture and development of Claude Code, Anthropic’s agentic CLI tool for complex coding tasks, terminal operations, and repository-wide edits through natural language.
- [Grounding your AI Agents with Google Search and Maps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5h-bqNpcqvU): This session shows how to use Vertex AI to ground Gemini models with real-time Google Search and Maps data, reducing hallucinations and building location-aware agents for geospatial queries.
- [Common Mistakes With Vibe Coded Websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNSXlBmukck): This Y Combinator design review highlights pitfalls in AI-generated "vibe-coded" websites, such as poor accessibility, broken mobile layouts, and missing metadata, with advice for production-ready apps.

## More

- [Looker's Product Roadmap for Trusted AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHqALWJG9oE): Google Cloud outlines the roadmap for Looker’s AI integration, focusing on conversational analytics and automated LookML generation that allow data developers to build governed, natural-language-ready data platforms.
