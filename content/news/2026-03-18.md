- [3 Advanced AI agent design patterns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89KKm_a4M7A): This Google Cloud Tech video explores three advanced design patterns for building effective AI agents.
- [Personal Intelligence expansion](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personal-intelligence-expansion/): Google expands Personal Intelligence features across Search, Gemini, and Chrome to provide more tailored and relevant responses based on personal context.
