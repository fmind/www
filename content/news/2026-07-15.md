- [Why the AI Industry Still Pays a "Python Tax"](https://hackernoon.com/why-the-ai-industry-still-pays-a-python-tax): The article explores the performance cost of Python in the AI industry and discusses how compiled languages like Swift and Rust are emerging to bridge the execution gap for on-device and low-latency AI.

- [Information et IA : audition de Google, OpenAI et Anthropic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3nilDZ33HPY): Representatives from Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic testified before the French Senate on the impact of generative AI, addressing concerns about disinformation, hallucinations, and their partnerships with media publishers.
