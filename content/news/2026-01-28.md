- [Introducing Agentic Vision in Gemini 3 Flash - Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/agentic-vision-gemini-3-flash/): Google introduces Agentic Vision in Gemini 3 Flash, combining visual reasoning with code execution so models investigate and manipulate images to ground answers in visual evidence.

- [Take your notebooks further by adding ... - Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/01/take-notebooks-further-notebooklm-gemini.html?m=1): Google Workspace users can now integrate NotebookLM notebooks as a source within the Gemini app, enabling deeper and more relevant responses grounded in their document collections for Enterprise and Education users.

- [MCP and Skills: Why Not Both? · kvg.dev](https://kvg.dev/posts/20260125-skills-and-mcp/): This article argues that MCP and Skills are complementary: MCP addresses the N×M integration problem for agents, and Skills solve context saturation by organizing local knowledge.
- [MCP is Not the Problem, It's your Server: Best Practices for Building ...](https://www.philschmid.de/mcp-best-practices): This article argues that MCP is not failing; developers build MCP servers as REST API wrappers instead of interfaces for AI agents, and it gives six best practices.
- [Clawdbot AI assistant: What it is, how to try it | Mashable](https://mashable.com/article/what-is-clawdbot-how-to-try): Clawdbot, an open-source AI personal assistant that runs locally and can access user data, has gone viral for its agentic capabilities but has since been renamed Moltbot due to trademark pressure from Anthropic.
- [AI Mode in Google Search and AI Overviews get Gemini upgrades](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-ai-overviews-updates/): Google Search is rolling out AI Mode and AI Overviews powered by Gemini 3, letting users ask complex questions and follow up conversationally directly from AI Overviews.
- [Better suggestions for meeting times ... - Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/01/improved-meeting-suggestions-gemini-calendar.html): Gemini in Google Calendar now offers improved meeting time suggestions by analyzing attendee availability, working hours, and potential conflicts, and also simplifies rescheduling meetings when multiple attendees decline.
- ['Dear Upstairs Neighbors': Animated film made with Google AI](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/dear-upstairs-neighbors/): "Dear Upstairs Neighbors," an animated short film created with Google AI, premiered at the Sundance Film Festival, showcasing how generative AI tools can assist animators while preserving artistic control.
